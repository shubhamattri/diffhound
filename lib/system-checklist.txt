## System understanding, architectural fit, reuse and maintainability
Before proposing a change, trace the existing caller, guards, state, consumer and later lifecycle.
Use the repository context supplied here; you cannot run tools or invent search/test results.
Look for existing helpers and settings. A reuse suggestion must name the implementation and
show compatible behavior, authorization, side effects and lifecycle, not just a similar name.
Identify misplaced responsibility by a concrete consequence and an existing boundary.
Check unused functions/imports/branches, duplicate implementations, redundant state,
speculative abstractions, misleading comments and tests that do not exercise the changed path.
For dead code, consider exports, decorators, registration, reflection and external consumers;
zero search hits in bounded context is insufficient. For ineffective tests, trace the assertions
through the changed function; do not equate sleeps or mocks with a vacuous test.
Prefer removal or reuse when it preserves behavior. Do not invent layers or style complaints.
Show the source evidence and the counterexample you ruled out. If evidence is incomplete,
mark the claim UNVERIFIABLE. Do not claim execution, production state or mutation results.
